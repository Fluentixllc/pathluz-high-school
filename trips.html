Fluentix Trips — learn by going there

Trips is a separate Fluentix product from Pathways. Pathways guides all year, Trips makes one experience real, a subject trip, a language trip, a university visit, or a career taster, tied to what was taught, with reflection before and after.

Draft product, not live, not a booking. No trip is offered here, no destination, date, provider, or cost is confirmed, and nothing is booked, charged, or guaranteed on this page.

Trips, on their own

Every language

Spanish, Portuguese, French in Phase 1, and any language added later, each with its own trip, local guides and hosts, workplace visits, and reflection with a teacher.

Every subject

History to historic places, science to labs and natural sites, maths to engineering and building, arts to museums and studios, professions to workplaces, each tied to what was taught.

University experiences

Workplace visits, industry talks, research projects, and mentoring, tied to a career pursued, with supervision, learning goals, and reflection. No placement, internship, or job is promised.

Career-change tasters

Taster sessions, workplace shadowing, short guided courses, and conversations with professionals, plus a clear look at the required check or qualification for that field. No new career or job is promised.

How Trips makes its own money, separately

Trips would be charged per trip, per student, for planning, preparation, reflection materials, and coordination with the licensed travel provider who actually runs the trip. That is different from Pathways, which would be a recurring school or teacher licence for year-round guidance.

Pricing is still to be confirmed. No price goes live without Maya's finance check and Miguel's approval, and Maya has not yet run Trips-specific per-trip costs, provider, supervision, transport, or safety costs, so no Trips price or margin is claimed here. Trips are run by a licensed travel provider, not by Fluentix or Pathluz alone, with school, teacher, and parent approval first.

Pathways and Trips, together, not forced together

A school can use Pathways all year and never book a trip. A family can join a trip without a full year of Pathways. Together, a trip participant gets a short Pathways pathway before and after the trip, and a Pathways student gets a real experience to reflect on, without either product depending on the other to survive.
