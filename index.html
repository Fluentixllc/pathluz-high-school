Your steps after secondary school

A separate version for high school students, made to fit into the Fluentix Ecosystem. It is a sequence of events, not a shortcut, and not a promise of a job, a place, or a result.

Draft version, not live. For high school students aged 14 and over, in line with Fluentix's 14+ rule. Prices, timelines, providers, and school-specific details are to be confirmed and are shown as “To be confirmed” where they would appear.

The sequence, in order

01

Discover

Explore professions in plain language. What does the work actually involve day to day, and what questions should you ask?

02

Learn

Learn, through Fluentix-style tutoring and guides, what a required check or application usually asks for, and why.

03

Prepare

Gather, in your own time, the details you will need. No data is collected on this draft page.

04

Check

Walk through the must-have check step by step, with support and encouragement at each stage.

05

Apply

When you are ready, apply through the official route for that profession. This draft does not submit an application.

06

Next steps

Receive your next steps, review what you learned, and keep your progress for the next stage, in school or beyond.

How it fits into Fluentix

Fluentix already works as Tutor, Classroom, and Grader on one login, for students aged 14 and over, with accounts closed to new sign-ups and a waitlist in place on the live site. This high school Pathluz sequence is designed to sit alongside that ecosystem, as a guided pathway after secondary school, not as a replacement for a teacher, a school, or a qualified adviser.

It educates, guides, supports, and encourages, and provides simple tools: a step sequence, plain-language explanations, and a checklist you can use with a teacher or parent. It does not collect student data on this draft, does not show prices, plans, or quotas to students, and does not make claims about outcomes.

Cost: To be confirmed. Provider: To be confirmed. Timeline: To be confirmed. Nothing on this draft page is an offer, a quote, a booking, or a guarantee.

Apply it on an educational trip, in every language and every subject

Every language we teach should be offered the opportunity to apply that knowledge with a real trip, and so should every other class and subject. Knowledge sticks when you use it, in real conversations, real places, and real work, not just in a classroom.

That means, for each language, including Spanish, Portuguese, and French in Phase 1, and any language added later: using that language with local guides and hosts, and visiting workplaces connected to a profession you are exploring.

And for other subjects, a trip related to that subject: history to historic places, science to labs and natural sites, maths to engineering and building, arts to museums and studios, professions to workplaces, and so on, always tied to what was actually taught, and always reflecting, with a teacher, on what you learned and what surprised you.

Connections with educational travel would be made through licensed travel providers, with teacher and parent supervision, clear safety plans, and no booking on this draft page. Provider, destination, dates, and cost are to be confirmed for each trip, and nothing here is a trip offer, a price, or a guarantee of a place.

This is a learning connection, not a travel sale. Any future trip would be run by a licensed travel provider, not by Pathluz or Fluentix alone, and would need school, teacher, and parent approval first.

At university level: experiences related to your career of choice

The sequence does not stop at secondary school. At university level, students should also be offered opportunities to have experiences related to their career of choice, not just classroom learning about it.

That could look like: workplace visits, industry talks, research projects, mentoring by professionals, and, where a university or employer offers them, internships or placements. Each experience should be tied to the career the student is actually pursuing, with supervision, clear learning goals, and reflection afterwards.

No placement, internship, job, or outcome is promised here. Provider, employer, dates, and cost are to be confirmed for each experience, and any future experience would need university, teacher, and, where applicable, parent approval first, and would be run by the university or employer offering it, not by Pathluz or Fluentix alone.

For teachers and parents
