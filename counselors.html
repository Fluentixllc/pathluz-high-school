A tool for counselors, to complement your work

Fluentix Pathways does not replace a career counselor or a school counselor. It gives you a clear, calm way to guide a student, track a pathway, and help with applications, step by step.

Draft tool, not live. No real student data is shown here, nothing is submitted, and nothing is saved on this draft page. School student data would only be used after a signed data agreement, under Fluentix's 14+ rules.

What this tool helps you do

Start a conversation

Use Discover and Learn to ask: What are you curious about? What did you enjoy learning? What surprised you? The pathway gives you a shared map, not a test score.

See the pathway, at a glance

Discover, Learn, Prepare, Check, Apply, Next steps, plus Trips, University experiences, and Career-change exploration, all in order, so you and the student always know where you are.

Help with applications

Use the Prepare and Check steps as an application checklist: what is usually asked for, what is ready, what is still to be confirmed, and what the official route is. This tool does not submit an application for a student.

Explore trips and experiences

Point to a subject-related trip, a university experience, or a career-change taster, each with teacher and parent approval first, provider and cost to be confirmed, and no booking here.

Encourage, don't rank

Progress is shown by step completed, not by ranking students against each other. No student prices, plans, or quotas are shown to students.

Hand over simple tools

Plain-language checklists, Help answers, and the Virtual Fair, which you can use in a 1:1 session, in a classroom, or send home to parents.

How it complements Fluentix

Tutor teaches the language or subject. Classroom lets a teacher assign and pause pathway steps. Grader assesses reflection and portfolio work a student produces, with teacher approval, not a career choice. Pathways connects those three into a sequence you can use with a student, from first curiosity to a trip, a university experience, or a career change.

This tool complements, it does not replace, professional counseling, school guidance, or advice from a qualified professional for a student's own situation. No job, place, licence, or outcome is promised.

Try it, in a session

1. Open the Virtual Fair together, and let the student pick a stall that catches their eye. 2. Go to Discover and Learn for that path. 3. Use Prepare and Check as an application checklist. 4. Agree one next step, and one thing to explore, a trip, a university experience, or a new field, with approval first.
