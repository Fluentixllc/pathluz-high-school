Welcome to the Virtual Fair

Explore career paths the fun way, stall by stall. Each stall is a place to learn, not a job offer.

Illustrated fair only, not a real job fair. No real employers, no job listings, no hiring, and no largest-fair claim. Stalls are generic career paths, for learning, guidance, and tools only.

How to use the fair

1. Pick a stall that catches your eye. 2. Learn what that path usually involves. 3. Use the Guide and Tools for that path. 4. If you want a real experience, look for a subject-related trip, a university experience, or a career-change taster, each with approval first, and each to be confirmed.

Cost, provider, dates, and places are to be confirmed for any real trip or experience. Nothing on this page is a job, a place, a booking, or a guarantee.
